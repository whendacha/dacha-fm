Dacha FM Privacy

Updated September 26, 2026. This policy covers Dacha FM version 1.0.0, including TestFlight builds. Individual hidden songs are supported from build 5; differences for earlier builds are described below.

On your iPhone

Favorites, playlists, hidden songs, and hidden artists are stored in the app's files. Your verified wallet identifier and cloud session token are stored in the system Keychain. The guest library stays on your device. In build 2, wallet profiles are also stored locally. Build 3 and later offer optional cloud sync after a separate confirmation in Meteor.

Cloud library

When you enable cloud sync, Dacha FM sends your public wallet account ID, favorites, playlists, their order and names, and hidden songs and artists to its service hosted on Supabase. The service stores track information and links; audio files are not uploaded. The database is located in the United States (us-east-2). Access to a library requires a valid session for its owner.

To sign in, the server verifies your public key and the signature of a one-time message. The database stores only a hash of the random session token. A session lasts up to 30 days, or until you sign out or delete your account. Unused sign-in requests expire after 5 minutes; expired service records are cleaned up during later sign-in requests. Your cloud account and library remain stored until you delete them. The app does not record tokens or signatures in its own logs.

Operational logs

Supabase and its network infrastructure retain operational logs that include your IP address, approximate city and country derived from that address, device or browser information sent in HTTP headers, request details and status codes, request durations, and network fingerprints used for security checks. These records support service operation, troubleshooting and abuse prevention. Dacha FM does not request access to your device's GPS location or use these records for advertising.

Network requests

The catalog loads from api.near.fm. Search text is sent to that service to find songs, and artist lookups also contact it. Music and images load from HTTPS addresses provided by the source, including main.fastfs.io. These services receive your IP address and information about the request, including the requested search, song or image. We have not verified how long these external services retain search or media request logs. Your personal library and wallet keys are not sent to them.

Signing in opens Meteor Wallet. The app receives your public identifier, public key, and the signature of a one-time message. Key ownership is checked through rpc.mainnet.fastnear.com, which receives the public account ID and public key. The app does not receive your recovery phrase or private keys, and it does not initiate transfers.

The sign-in page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in its Pages documentation. The signature returns to the app in a URL fragment. Dacha FM has no analytics SDK or advertising tracking of its own.

Deletion and contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email whendacha@gmail.com. Messages you send may include your contact details and information needed to answer your request. Do not include wallet recovery phrases, private keys or passwords.

Use Settings → Delete account to delete your cloud account. The server deletes its library and revokes all its sessions. The app then clears the local profile. Changes on other devices cannot reach the deleted account without a new sign-in. Previously saved local copies on other devices may remain until the local profile or app is deleted.

Account deletion does not immediately erase hosting providers' operational logs or technical backups. Those records follow the providers' separate retention policies; this policy does not promise a fixed number of retention days. Deleting a local build 2 profile clears only the data on that iPhone. It does not delete your wallet, public blockchain records, or records held by the music source. Contact and feedback options.
